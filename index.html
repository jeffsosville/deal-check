The "1.11x" lawn deal is a 3.4x deal

Deal No. 1 is Business Brokers of Florida MLS listing BBF-774-00002. Here's the thread next to the listing.

The $628K is 2025 revenue ($908,010) minus 2026 cost of goods ($280,218): gross profit from two different years, with $600K+ of operating expenses left out. On its 2025 tax return, the business earned $126,948.

What we found

  • The lead deal's "cash flow" is gross profit, not cash flow.The thread's No. 1, a Duval County lawn business at "1.11x," is BBF MLS listing BBF-774-00002. The thread's $628K matches 2025 revenue ($908,010) minus 2026 cost of goods ($280,218), which ignores more than $600K of 2025 operating expenses. The listing's actual discretionary earnings were $126,948 in 2025 and $133,011 on its current P&L. It asks $449,000, a 3.4x multiple.
  • These are real, broker-represented listings, but the broker isn't shown.All nine trace to a broker listing, eight on BizBuySell and BizQuest and one on the Florida BBF MLS. The cards in the thread route inquiries through the poster's platform instead.
  • "Listed a week ago" isn't accurate for most of them.The glass glazing listing dates to about June 2025, the medical practice to about late June 2026, and the print shop to about early September. Only the legal-services listing is under a month old.
  • Several numbers don't match the source listing.The daycare's asking price, the network company's asking price, and the bakery's and print shop's revenue all differ from what the listing shows today.
  • Some headline figures are hard to reconcile.A collision shop with $1.4M revenue, $240K annual rent, and 12 employees is shown producing $809K of cash flow. The lawn business's 69% "margin" is a gross margin. Its 2025 tax return shows a 14% earnings margin.

How we did this

  • Matching. Each card's asking price and cash flow were matched against the DealLedger index (broker-direct, BizBuySell, and BizQuest tables), then confirmed by title on BizBuySell, BizQuest, BusinessBroker.net, BizBen, and broker sites.
  • Days on market. For listings DealLedger has tracked, we use its recorded listing date. For newer listings (Nos. 3, 4, 8), we estimated the date from the BizBuySell listing ID. BBS IDs are assigned sequentially, at roughly 10,000 per three weeks in mid-2026. The print-shop estimate was cross-checked against its BusinessBroker.net ID. Treat estimates as ±2 weeks.
  • Views. "BizQuest views" are the listing-view counters on each BizQuest page, read Oct 26, 2026. "Thread viewed" is the "buyers viewed this" number printed on each card in the thread.
  • Financials. All revenue and cash-flow figures are seller-reported and unverified by us, the brokers, or the marketplaces. Where sources disagree, we show each figure.
